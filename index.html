What makes our tutoring work

Twice a week

Kids meet with a tutor two times every week. Frequent practice is what builds lasting reading skills.

Standards-based

Every lesson is matched to Utah’s reading standards for your child’s grade.

Five core skills

Lessons cover the five skills research shows every reader needs, from sounds to understanding stories.

Step-by-step lessons

Tutors follow a written plan for every session, so each visit builds on the last.

A program built for young readers

35 min

sessions, twice every week

In the first session of the week, the tutor teaches one reading skill and practices it with your child. In the second, your child uses that skill on their own with new words and short stories.

What your child will practice

Strong reading takes five skills working together. Every grade’s lessons cover all five.

Phonemic awareness

Hearing the separate sounds inside spoken words.

Phonics

Matching letters to sounds to sound out new words.

Fluency

Reading smoothly, at a steady pace, with expression.

Vocabulary

Learning the words that make harder books make sense.

Comprehension

Understanding, retelling, and talking about what they read.
