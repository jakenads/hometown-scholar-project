What your child will practice

Strong reading takes five skills working together. Every grade’s lessons cover all five.

Phonemic awareness

Hearing the separate sounds inside spoken words.

Phonics

Matching letters to sounds to sound out new words.

Fluency

Reading smoothly, at a steady pace, with expression.

Vocabulary

Learning the words that make harder books make sense.

Comprehension

Understanding, retelling, and talking about what they read.

Why we started

Hometown Scholars Project was started by Jacob Nadauld and William Penrod, two Springville kids who grew up here and graduated from Springville High School.

While doing community service as we prepared for medical school, we realized there had to be a better way to help the kids in the community that raised us. One-time service projects weren’t enough. Kids learning to read need steady, one-on-one help, week after week.

So we built it: free, twice-weekly reading tutoring, right here in our hometown.

What we ask of tutors

  • 18 or older
  • Pass a Springville City background check
  • Be a strong reader. No training or teaching experience needed.
  • Two 35-minute sessions a week with the same student

Questions families ask

Does it cost anything?

No. Tutoring is completely free.

What do you ask of families?

Our tutors are volunteers who set aside their evenings for your child, so we ask families to come to both weekly sessions. If you need to miss one, please let us know at least 24 hours ahead. After two missed sessions without notice, we may offer your time slot to another family.

Where and when are sessions held?

At the Springville Public Library (45 S Main St), year-round, by appointment. Sessions run Monday through Thursday from 5 to 9 pm, and Fridays from 5 to 6 pm.

What can I do at home?

Encourage your child to read the book they picked out with their tutor. That matters more than anything. If you can, also help them with the short practice activities they bring home.

Who are the tutors?

Volunteers from our community, all 18 or older, who have passed a Springville City background check. They follow a written lesson plan for every session.

Which grades do you serve?

Kindergarten through 5th grade, with lessons written for each grade.

What happens after I sign up?

We’ll contact you to set up regular session times that work for your family.

How do you protect my child’s information?

Everything you share with us is kept strictly confidential. It is never shared with anyone except registered volunteers who have passed a Springville City background check.

How do I get in touch?

Email info@hometownscholarsproject.org.

Ready to get started?

Fill out a short form with your child’s name and grade, the best way to reach you, and the times that work best for your family. We’ll take it from there.
